## Full description (≤4000 chars)
**FluentMate** is your AI-powered English speaking companion, built for Vietnamese learners. Talk to a friendly AI coach in realistic role-play scenarios and get instant grammar corrections, pronunciation feedback, and natural rephrasing — right inside the conversation.

**Why FluentMate?**

🎙️ **Speak naturally, get corrected instantly**
Tap the mic and chat with AI in real-life scenarios: ordering coffee, job interviews, daily standups, meeting new colleagues, restaurant reservations, and more. The AI listens, responds, points out grammar mistakes, and suggests more native phrasings.

🎯 **Personalised to your level**
During onboarding, FluentMate asks about your goals, industry, English level (A1–C1), and difficulty preference. The AI adjusts pace, vocabulary, and sentence complexity to match you.

📊 **Detailed report after every session**
End each chat with a full breakdown:
• Fluency, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary scores (0–100)
• Your best sentence of the session
• Mispronounced words with IPA + Vietnamese tips
• Strengths and areas to improve
• Personal encouragement message

📅 **Daily streaks & 60-second review**
Keep your daily streak, and review vocabulary flashcards using the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm. Just 60 seconds a day to retain what you learn.

🌐 **Instant translation**
Stuck on a phrase? Tap the translate button to switch between Vietnamese ↔ English mid-conversation.

🔒 **Safe & private**
Data is encrypted in transit. You can delete your account and all history anytime, right from inside the app.

**Who is it for?**
• Students and office workers who need English at work
• Long-time learners who freeze up when speaking
• IELTS / TOEIC Speaking candidates
• Anyone who wants to speak English more confidently every day

**Requirements**
Microphone access for speaking practice. Internet connection to talk to the AI.
