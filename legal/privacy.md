## English

This Privacy Policy describes how **FluentMate** ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

### 1. Information we collect

When you use FluentMate, we collect:

- **Account information**: email address, display name (you provide on signup).
- **Audio recordings**: when you tap the microphone in conversation practice, your voice is recorded and sent to our speech-to-text provider for transcription. We do not retain raw audio after transcription is complete.
- **Conversation transcripts**: text from your spoken practice + AI replies, stored to power session reports and progress tracking.
- **Vocabulary progress**: spaced-repetition data (cards reviewed, quality scores, intervals).
- **Usage data**: minutes practiced, sessions completed, feature interactions.
- **Device data**: language preference, OS version (for app stability).

We do **not** collect contacts, photos, location, advertising IDs, or browsing history.

### 2. How we use your information

- Run AI conversation, pronunciation scoring, and language transcription.
- Generate end-of-session reports.
- Track learning progress (streaks, daily goals).
- Improve the App.

### 3. Third-party services we share data with

| Service | Data shared | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase (USA) | Account info, transcripts, progress data | Backend storage + authentication | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| Google Gemini API (USA) | Conversation text, transcripts, scenario context | AI conversation replies, grammar feedback, session reports, translation | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google Cloud Speech-to-Text (USA) | Audio recordings | Speech-to-text transcription | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (USA) | Text of AI replies | Voice playback (text-to-speech) | https://policies.google.com/privacy |

We do **not** sell your personal information to anyone. We do not use your data to train any AI model.

### 4. Data retention & deletion

- Conversation history is retained as long as your account exists.
- You can **delete your account in-app** at any time: **Profile → Account & plan → Delete account**. This permanently removes all of your data from our database within 30 days.
- You can also request deletion via the web form: **https://fluentmate.app/delete-account.html** or by emailing **shopbebe56@gmail.com**.

### 5. Children's privacy

FluentMate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided personal information, please contact **shopbebe56@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

### 6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / Vietnam Decree 13/2023)

You have the right to:
- Access the data we hold about you.
- Request correction or deletion of your data.
- Withdraw consent at any time.
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, email **shopbebe56@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days.
